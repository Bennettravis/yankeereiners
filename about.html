About YRHA

A reining community built around horses, riders, and honest competition.

YRHA is more than a show — it is a western sport community built around quality runs, respected judging, and a rider-first experience from check-in to championship day.

What we stand for

Fair competition, strong values, and a horse-first culture.

From first-time competitors to seasoned riders, YRHA creates a welcoming environment where skill, professionalism, and respect are part of every run.

  • Thoughtful judging and clear event standards
  • A competitive atmosphere that still feels welcoming
  • Support from a tight-knit reining community

What members get

A place to compete, connect, and keep improving.

Our shows are designed to give riders the kind of experience that encourages growth, confidence, and return visits year after year.

About the show

Built for horse and rider excellence.

Yankee Reining Horse Association sets the standard for reining competition in the Northeast. From challenging arena conditions and sharp judging to a strong field of competitors and passionate spectators, every YRHA event is built around quality, consistency, and commitment to the sport.

Whether you're chasing NRHA affiliate titles, year-end high-point honors, or a standout run in a competitive draw, YRHA gives horses and riders the opportunity to perform at their best.

✓High-quality arena footing and judging
✓Family-friendly spectator experience

About YRHA

Competition built on skill, integrity, and tradition.

Yankee Reining Horse Association is dedicated to advancing the sport with high-quality events, fair competition, and a strong rider community across the Northeast.

Our mission

We raise the standard for reining.

From entry-level competitors to seasoned veterans, YRHA creates a competitive environment where horses and riders can grow, perform, and celebrate the sport.

  • Professional-level judging and arena management
  • Supportive, family-friendly event atmosphere
  • Strong community connection between members and sponsors

About the show

Built for horse and rider excellence.

Yankee Reining Horse Association sets the standard for reining competition in the Northeast. From challenging arena conditions and sharp judging to a strong field of competitors and passionate spectators, every YRHA event is built around quality, consistency, and commitment to the sport.

Whether you're chasing NRHA affiliate titles, year-end high-point honors, or a standout run in a competitive draw, YRHA gives horses and riders the opportunity to perform at their best.

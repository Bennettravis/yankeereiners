About the show

Built for horse and rider excellence.

Yankee Reining Horse Association sets the standard for reining competition in the Northeast. From challenging arena conditions and sharp judging to a strong field of competitors and passionate spectators, every YRHA event is built around quality, consistency, and commitment to the sport.

Whether you're chasing NRHA affiliate titles, year-end high-point honors, or a standout run in a competitive draw, YRHA gives horses and riders the opportunity to perform at their best.

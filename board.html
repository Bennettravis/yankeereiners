Leadership

Guiding the association with experience and direction.

YRHA leadership is focused on strong event execution, member support, and upholding the standards of reining competition.

Board focus

Steady leadership for a growing sport.

Our board members work to support riders, maintain event quality, and keep the association rooted in fairness and community.

  • Event planning and execution
  • Membership support and communication
  • Competition standards and continuity

Leadership values

Experience, accountability, and service.

The board is committed to building a long-term culture of respect, professionalism, and participant-first competition.
